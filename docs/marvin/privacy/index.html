The short version

Marvin is a private, self-hosted WhatsApp assistant. The production workflow currently consults Google Calendar events. The standard OAuth credential requests broader calendar permissions, including write access. Future create, change and cancel operations are planned and are not implemented. Gmail and Google Tasks are not part of the documented configuration. Message text is processed by Anthropic, and voice notes are transcribed by Groq, according to the deployment documentation. Conversation history is described as stored on private infrastructure; retention and deletion are not documented here.

The intended commitments are not to sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train models. These commitments require verification, including the providers’ own practices. This draft is not a claim that Google has reviewed or approved Marvin.

1. About this policy

Marvin is a private, self-hosted WhatsApp assistant operated as part of the Muritiba Homelab. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the operator of wmuritiba.com who runs this deployment. “Marvin” refers to the application, its WhatsApp interface and the related automation workflows.

This policy applies to personal information and Google user data handled by Marvin. It does not apply to this static website, which sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It also does not apply to Google, WhatsApp, Anthropic, Groq or any other third-party service. Those services handle data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Marvin is not a public service. It is not operated by, endorsed by or affiliated with Google, WhatsApp, Meta, Anthropic or Groq.

2. Who can use Marvin

The deployment documentation describes Marvin as available only to the operator and authorised family members, through a private WhatsApp connection. The access model and the absence of a reachable application endpoint are not documented here.

Marvin is a private family tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended for use by children without adult supervision. It is not an emergency service, a medical service or a substitute for professional advice.

If you are an authorised user, you should only connect or query calendars that you are entitled to access. Do not use Marvin to access another person’s calendar without their permission.

3. Google data we access

Marvin connects to Google Calendar through OAuth. The Google Account is authorised by the operator; Marvin does not ask end users to sign in with Google. The production workflow currently queries calendar events. The standard OAuth credential requests calendar and calendar.events, which permit broader access, including write operations. The scopes effectively granted have not been consulted directly. There are no Gmail or Google Tasks integrations in the documented production workflow. Creating, changing and cancelling events through natural-language WhatsApp commands is planned for a future stage and is not currently implemented.

Data Source Why it is accessed
Calendar event details
Event titles, descriptions, start and end times, time zone, location and other fields returned by the Google Calendar API.		 Google Calendar API To answer schedule questions from authorised users, such as what is happening on a particular day or when the next event starts.
Calendar metadata
Calendar names and identifiers for the calendars the operator has connected.		 Google Calendar API To select the correct personal or family calendar for a query and to avoid querying calendars that were not connected.
Connected account identifier
The Google Account email address associated with the authorised calendar connection.		 Google OAuth To identify and manage the connection, and to keep the credential associated with the correct account.

Calendar events can include information about other people, such as attendees’ names or email addresses. They can also include sensitive details in event titles or descriptions. That information is handled in the same way as the operator’s own calendar data and may be processed by the providers described below.

What the documentation says Marvin does not access

  • Gmail, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Photos or other Google services, according to the documented configuration.
  • Calendar data from accounts that have not been authorised by the operator.
  • Google Sign-In for public users. Marvin is not described as a Google Sign-In application.
Current operations and planned scope

The production workflow currently queries calendar events. The standard OAuth credential requests broader calendar permissions, including write access. Creating, changing and cancelling events through WhatsApp is planned and will require appropriate authorisation. The exact granted scopes, the write behaviour and any future scope such as calendar.events are not documented here.

4. How we use data

We use the data described in this policy to:

  • Receive and interpret messages sent to Marvin through WhatsApp.
  • Read the connected Google Calendars when a request is about the schedule.
  • Generate and send a reply in the same WhatsApp conversation.
  • Maintain conversation context so that follow-up questions make sense.
  • Operate, troubleshoot, secure and maintain the deployment.
  • Respond to requests about access, correction or deletion.
  • Comply with applicable law.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, retargeting, profiling, creditworthiness or lending decisions. We do not sell it, rent it or provide it to data brokers. We do not use it to train machine-learning models. These are intended commitments and require verification.

Where a message includes information about another person, the operator is responsible for having an appropriate basis to share that information with Marvin and its processors.

5. AI providers and WhatsApp

Marvin is self-hosted, but it relies on external services to deliver its features. The following providers may process message content, relevant calendar details and technical metadata:

Provider Role Data that may be processed
Anthropic Language model provider. Processes messages, prompts and recent conversation history. Message text, relevant calendar details, conversation context and prompts needed to produce a response.
Groq Speech-to-text provider for voice notes. Audio is transcribed by Groq Whisper. Audio content of voice notes, and the resulting transcription. The documentation states that the transcription service handles audio in memory and does not write it to disk.
WhatsApp / Meta Messaging platform used to send and receive messages. Message content, phone numbers, timestamps and other metadata handled by WhatsApp. This is governed by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.
Local Ollama model (documented) A local Ollama fallback is described in the project documentation; whether it is enabled is not stated here. Message text and conversation context, if the local fallback is active.

The intended commitments are not to sell Google user data or use it for advertising. The external AI providers are documented as processing data to provide the requested feature; their roles, retention and model-training practices require separate review. Their handling is governed by their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Groq Privacy Policy
  • WhatsApp Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Google Calendar query results are formatted locally in the direct flow. Whether those responses can reappear in conversation history is not documented. Certain sensitive patterns, such as credentials, tokens and payment-card numbers, are redacted in messages. This is not complete anonymisation and does not necessarily mask general personal data.

These providers may process data outside Australia. Their processing locations and retention practices are not documented, and we cannot promise that their handling matches ours.

6. Storage, security and retention

Where data is stored

According to the deployment documentation, Marvin’s application components run on private infrastructure controlled by the operator. Google OAuth credentials are described as stored in the self-hosted automation platform’s credential store, protected by its master key. Conversation history is described as stored in a self-hosted SQLite database, isolated per user. Calendar data retrieved to answer a request may appear in conversation history and in the automation platform’s execution records.

The deployment documentation describes a private access path rather than a public application endpoint. The actual access method, its encryption and the exposure of the service are not documented here. Access to the underlying server and data is described as restricted to the operator.

Retention

We retain data only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Conversation history is limited to 50 turns per user. It has no fixed expiration by time and may retain transcriptions.
  • Execution records are not configured for retention in the production n8n workflow. Tasks, logs and backups have no proven automatic expiration.
  • Calendar data is queried when needed. Whether it remains in conversation history or execution records is not documented.
  • Audio is processed by the transcription service, which does not write audio files to disk. WAHA removes local media after approximately 180 seconds. This is separate from any retention by Groq or WhatsApp.
  • Backups of the private infrastructure may contain copies of stored data. Their existence, contents and retention are not documented here.

Retention periods are reviewed when the deployment changes. We do not promise a specific deletion period for data that is outside our control, such as data held by WhatsApp, Anthropic or Groq.

Who can access stored data

Because Marvin is privately operated, the operator may access stored conversations, execution records and calendar data when necessary to operate, maintain, troubleshoot or secure the service, to respond to a request, or to comply with the law. Access is limited to what is necessary for those purposes. We do not permit anyone else to read Google user data except with affirmative consent, for security, or as required by law.

Security

The deployment documentation describes access restrictions, isolated container networks, private credentials and restricted remote access. These controls and the encryption in use are not independently verified here. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We share data only as described in this policy:

  • Service providers: Anthropic, Groq and WhatsApp are documented as processing data for messaging, transcription and language-model features. Their roles and terms require separate review.
  • Infrastructure: the private hosting environment used to run Marvin, limited to what is necessary to operate the service.
  • Security and abuse: where necessary to investigate a security incident, a bug or misuse.
  • Legal requirements: where required by applicable law, regulation or a valid legal process.
  • With consent: where you have given explicit consent to a specific disclosure.

We do not sell Google user data. We do not transfer it to advertising platforms, data brokers or information resellers. We do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. We do not allow humans to read Google user data except as described in the Limited Use section below.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use

Limited Use statement

Marvin’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In line with those requirements:

  • We limit our use of Google user data to providing and improving user-facing features that are prominent in Marvin’s interface — primarily answering calendar and schedule questions.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except to provide or improve those features, for security purposes, to comply with applicable law, or with the user’s explicit consent.
  • We do not allow humans to read Google user data unless the user has given affirmative agreement to view specific data, or the access is necessary for security, legal compliance, or aggregated internal operations.
  • We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, including retargeting, personalised advertising or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not use Google user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

This statement describes this deployment’s intended commitments. It is not a representation that Google has reviewed, approved or certified Marvin. Compliance with the Limited Use requirements has not been independently verified.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Provide access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or out of date.
  • Delete information we control, subject to lawful retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent for future processing.
  • Explain how your information has been handled.

To make a request, email beto.com2@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. The response process and timeframe are not documented. There is no self-service deletion through WhatsApp; deletion is administrative. The operator may refuse or delay deletion where required for security or legal reasons, but will not promise immediate or complete deletion of tasks, logs or backups.

You can revoke Marvin’s access to your Google Account at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Revoking access stops future calendar queries. It does not automatically delete conversation history or execution records already stored by Marvin; contact us to request deletion.

If you are in Australia and are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to raise a concern with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under applicable law.

10. Children

Marvin is a private family tool and is not offered to the public. It is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended for use by children without adult supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for a public service.

11. Changes to this policy

If a change involves a new use of Google user data, we will update this policy and, where required, ask the relevant users to consent before the new use begins. We will not use Google user data for a purpose that is not disclosed here.
