The short version

2. The service

Marvin receives messages through WhatsApp and currently consults connected Google Calendar events. Creating, changing and cancelling events through natural-language WhatsApp commands is planned for a future stage and is not implemented. The standard OAuth credential requests broader calendar permissions, including write access. Marvin uses language-model and transcription providers to generate replies. The service is self-hosted on private infrastructure and is not offered to the public.

Marvin is a personal and family tool. It is not a commercial service, a managed product, an emergency service or a substitute for professional advice. Features may be added, changed or removed at any time.

3. Eligibility and access

The deployment documentation describes access as limited to the operator and authorised family members. Whether a public sign-up exists is not documented. The operator may refuse, suspend or revoke access at any time, including for misuse or for security reasons.

You must be old enough to use WhatsApp and to understand these terms, or use Marvin under appropriate adult supervision. You must only connect or query calendars that you own or are authorised to access. You are responsible for keeping your device, WhatsApp account and Google Account secure.

5. Google services

Marvin uses Google Calendar through OAuth. The standard credential requests calendar and calendar.events, which permit broader access, including write operations. The scopes effectively granted have not been consulted directly. There are no Gmail or Google Tasks integrations in the documented production workflow. Your use of Google services is governed by the Google APIs Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Marvin is not endorsed by or affiliated with Google.

Calendar information can be incomplete, out of date or incorrect. You should verify important schedule information directly in Google Calendar or with the people involved. You can revoke Marvin’s access at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

The draft Marvin Privacy Policy is intended to describe the Google user data Marvin accesses, how it is used and the Limited Use commitments that would apply. The policy is not yet verified.

6. AI outputs and reliance

Marvin uses language models and speech-to-text services. According to the deployment documentation, Anthropic Claude Haiku processes messages, prompts and recent conversation history; Groq Whisper transcribes audio; and Ollama is implemented as a local fallback conditioned on availability. Google Calendar query results are formatted locally in the direct flow; whether those responses can reappear in conversation history is not documented. AI output can be wrong, incomplete, outdated or misinterpreted. A confident-sounding reply is not a guarantee that it is correct. You must independently verify important information.

Redaction of certain sensitive patterns exists in messages, but it is not complete anonymisation and does not necessarily mask general personal data. Marvin does not provide medical, legal, financial or other professional advice. Nothing it says should be treated as such advice. If you have an emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service rather than using Marvin.

We do not guarantee that Marvin will be available, timely, uninterrupted or error-free, or that any particular message will be processed.

Do not send information to Marvin that you are not comfortable having processed by those providers. Calendar descriptions and messages can contain sensitive information; avoid placing sensitive details in connected calendars if you do not want them processed.
