The short version

This is a private, operator-run deployment of open-source Inbox Zero v2.30.0. It requests broad Gmail OAuth scopes, including restricted Gmail scopes. The features currently enabled are narrower than the granted permissions. Use it only with Gmail accounts you are authorised to manage, verify AI output before acting on it, and do not rely on it for emergencies. The deployment documentation describes sending, deletion and bulk cleanup as disabled; the live settings are not documented. The service may change or stop, and it is provided without a guarantee of uptime.

6. AI outputs and email actions

Inbox Zero uses language models to classify email and support organisation. According to the deployment documentation, Anthropic Claude Haiku is the active provider, and Groq is configured as a fallback conditioned on the existence of a valid credential. If active, Groq may process the same email content. Sender, recipient, subject, date and textual content of messages may be sent to the AI provider. Attachments are not sent in the standard operation identified. Redaction of certain credentials, tokens and payment-card patterns is active by default, but the policy can be changed per account and general personal data is not necessarily masked. This is not complete anonymisation. AI output can be wrong, incomplete or based on a misunderstanding of context. Labels, rules and suggested actions may be incorrect. You must review important changes and verify information before relying on it.

The deployment documentation states that sending email, deletion, bulk cleanup, automatic draft creation and outbound webhook actions are disabled. These restrictions are documented, not independently verified. They reduce the scope of automated action, but they do not make AI output infallible.

Do not use this deployment for emergencies, legal or medical decisions, or any situation where an incorrect or delayed action could cause harm. It does not provide professional advice.

We do not guarantee that the deployment will be available, timely, uninterrupted or error-free, or that any rule will produce the result you expect.
